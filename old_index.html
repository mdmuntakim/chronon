✦ Quick Conversion Reference

From seconds to chronons

One Chronon is roughly one second. One second is roughly one-and-a-quarter chronons.

1 Chronon (1 Ch)0.8589934592 s
1 Standard Second1.1641532183 Ch
1 Standard Minute69.84919310 Ch
1 Standard Hour4,190.951586 Ch
1 Standard Day100,582.8381 Ch
— CHRONONS
$ chronon convert 3600
→ 4,190.951586 Ch // one hour

$ chronon convert 86400
→ 100,582.8381 Ch // one day
